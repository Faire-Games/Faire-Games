Four games worth keeping on your phone, in one app that never asks you for anything.

BREAKOUT
Clear the bricks. Power-ups widen the paddle, slow the ball, or let it smash straight through. Levels get faster.

FALLING BLOCKS
The one you already know how to play. Lines clear, the level climbs, the pieces come quicker.

SUDOKU
Puzzles at several difficulties, with the number pad where your thumb already is.

2048
Slide the tiles, watch them merge, chase your best score. Reaching 2048 is not the end if you do not want it to be.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

No advertising. No purchases. No account. No network access at all — every game runs entirely on your device. Your progress is saved when you leave a game and restored when you come back, including if the phone puts the app to sleep mid-round.

Open source, built with the Day framework: one Rust codebase drawing itself with each platform's real native widgets. github.com/daybrite/Day-Games
